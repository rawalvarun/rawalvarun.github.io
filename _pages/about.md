Software Engineer with 7+ years of experience building scalable, distributed systems. Currently **SDE 2 at Amazon Ads**, leading end-to-end development of full-stack features spanning backend services, APIs, React frontends, and cloud infrastructure on AWS.

I hold a **Master of Science in Machine Learning** from **Carnegie Mellon University** (2020) and a **B.Tech in Computer Science** from **IIT Kharagpur** (2017).

## What I Work On

At Amazon Ads, I own large-scale systems including an advertiser permission delegation platform, batch access control APIs, micro-frontend KPI widgets, and observability pipelines — contributing across ~18 packages from backend to CDK infrastructure.

Beyond my day job, I've built AI-powered products: a vector search recommendation engine at Zaytun AI, a Generative AI workflow automation system at HyperWater.ai, and a RAG-based medical AI platform as CTO of CeraMD.ai.

## Research & Publications

My research interests span AI/ML systems, computer vision, and NLP. I have publications at IEEE LCN and on arXiv, including work on frequency-based explanations for robust CNNs and network throughput fairness.
